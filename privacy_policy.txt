Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Personal Data
*************

While using Our Service, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally
identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify You.
Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  * Email address. Required to create an Account, to sign in with a one-time

  * Location information: latitude and longitude, PIN code, and a
    human-readable place name (see "Information Collected while Using the
    Application" below).

  * Support correspondence: the contents of emails You send Us, and any reason

Search, Comparison and Click Activity
*************************************

  * Search terms You type or dictate in the Service, including grocery,
    medicine and gift card searches, and Your recent-search list.

  * Which Marketplaces You have enabled, which comparison You viewed, and
    feedback You submit about incorrect product grouping.

  * Which product links You open, which Marketplace they pointed to, and the
    search term that produced them. For these click events We record the
    Marketplace, the destination URL, the search term and the IP address of
    the request. We use this to build the "trending searches" and "most
    visited stores" lists shown in the app, to detect abuse, and to reconcile
    affiliate commissions (see "Affiliate Links and Commissions").

  * Items You add to Your comparison cart or gift card cart.

Voice Input
***********

The Service offers voice search. When You tap the microphone icon:

  * Your device records audio only while the microphone indicator is active,
    and stops when You stop speaking or close the search field.

  * The audio is converted to text by the speech recognition service built
    into Your operating system (Apple Speech Recognition on iOS/macOS, the
    Android speech recognition service on Android, the browser's speech API on
    the web). Depending on Your device settings, that provider may process the
    audio on their servers under their own privacy policy.

  * We receive only the resulting text, which We then treat as a normal search
    term. We do not store, transmit or retain the audio recording.

  * You may deny or revoke microphone permission at any time in Your device
    settings; the rest of the Service continues to work.

Advertising and Analytics Identifiers

Unless You have an ad-free subscription, the Service uses advertising and

  * Your Advertising Identifier (Google Advertising ID / IDFA).

  * A Firebase Installation ID, generated by Google Firebase to identify Your

  * A Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token for Your device, so We

  * A device identifier associated with any price alerts You create, so the
    right device is notified.

  * Cookies and similar web storage on the web version (see "Cookies and Local
    Storage").

Cryptocurrency and Financial Data
*********************************

ShopSwiftly provides a platform for mobile recharge and gift card purchases
paid for with cryptocurrency.

  * Non-Custodial Service: We do not create user wallets, nor do We hold
    custody of user funds or private keys.

  * Payment Processing: Payments are processed by third-party payment
    gateways, currently MixPay (and, where offered, Binance Pay). You are
    taken to the gateway's own hosted payment page. We do not receive or store
    Your card number, wallet credentials, seed phrase or private keys.

  * What We do store about a payment: the order reference, the payment method
    and currency, the expected and paid amounts, the gateway's payment link,
    the on-chain transaction hash where the gateway supplies one, and the
    payment status. A transaction hash is public blockchain data and may be
    linkable to Your wallet by anyone; please consider this before paying.

  * Gift card fulfilment data: for a completed order We store the issued gift
    card details needed to deliver and support it — which can include the card
    code or ePIN, voucher ID, redemption URL, expiry date, redemption
    instructions and provider confirmation number. This is sensitive; treat
    Your order history like You would a wallet.

  * The payment page may ask for camera access (for example to scan a QR code
    to pay). That request comes from the payment provider inside a secure web
    view. We do not receive, view or store any image or video from Your
    camera.

  * We do not perform Know Your Customer (KYC) checks ourselves; the payment
    gateway may do so under its own policy.

Information Collected while Using the Application
*************************************************

While using Our Application, in order to provide features of Our Application,
We may collect, with Your prior permission:

  * Location — precise location (GPS) or, if You prefer, a PIN code or place
    name You enter manually.

  * Microphone and speech recognition — for voice search only (see "Voice
    Input").

  * Notifications — permission to display push notifications.

  * Camera — only if the payment provider's hosted checkout page requests it
    during payment (see above).

How We use Your location: Marketplace prices, availability and delivery
eligibility in India are local. We use Your latitude and longitude, or Your
PIN code, to determine the serviceable store for each Marketplace and to
request prices for that store. To do this We:

  * Send Your coordinates or PIN code to Google Maps Platform (Places and
    Geocoding APIs), through Our own server, to convert between coordinates,
    PIN codes and place names, and to power location autocomplete.

  * Send Your coordinates, PIN code and/or the resolved store identifier to
    the relevant Marketplace's public search interface, together with Your
    search term, so it can return prices for Your area. For some Marketplaces
    this request is made by Our servers on Your behalf; for others it is made
    directly from Your device, in which case that Marketplace also receives
    Your IP address and device network information.

  * Store Your latitude, longitude, PIN code, place name and the resolved
    store identifiers on Your device so You do not have to set Your location
    on every visit.

  * Store Your latitude, longitude and PIN code on Our servers as part of any
    price alert You create, because We need them to re-check the price for
    Your area.

You can enable or disable access to location, microphone, camera and
notifications at any time through Your Device settings. If You decline
location, You can still use the Service by entering a PIN code manually.

  * Price-drop alerts You have asked for.

Price Alerts
------------

If You create a price alert, We store: Your email address, a device identifier
for the device to notify, the product identifier and name, the Marketplace,
the price You want to be alerted at, the check frequency, and Your latitude,
longitude, PIN code and store identifiers (needed to re-check the price for
Your area). We record each price check We perform for that alert, so We can
show You price history and avoid duplicate notifications.

Alerts expire automatically (by default three days after creation, or when
triggered), and are deleted when You delete Your Account.

Retention of Your Personal Data
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Company will retain Your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary
